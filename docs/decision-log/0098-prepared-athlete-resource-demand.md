# 0098 — Prepared athlete-specific resource demand

Date: 2026-09-10

Status: accepted

Decision version: `prepared-resource-demand@1.0.0`

## Decision

Add a server-prepared, content-addressed resource-demand candidate for the first owner-alpha
muscular-endurance strategy. The projection uses only one exact DEVELOP priority, the ratified
resource-authority bundle, an active planning-reviewer assignment, and point-in-time factual
environment state. An environment is eligible only when the exact stable chair is reported
available and at least 2 m² of usable floor space is recorded.

The server previews the existing deterministic resolver and offers a candidate only when chair
sit-to-stand is a FULL match. Acceptance sends deterministic UUIDv5 identities into the existing
atomic preparation service, which appends one stimulus requirement, resolution, resource demand,
and decision record. Exact retries return that chain; partial identity occupation, unrelated demand
history, changed authority, and changed environment state fail closed.

Reserve ten minutes per week across two five-minute slots. Twice-weekly frequency is within the
reviewed ACSM claim. The ten-minute amount is a deliberately small, replaceable engineering
scheduling envelope, not an evidence-derived training dose. It establishes no sets, repetitions,
effort, tempo, rest, progression, stop rules, or authority to perform a session.

## Reason

The prior screen correctly exposed every expert input but required the owner to author stimulus
ontology, select policy IDs, choose an exercise, interpret evidence, and enter resource values.
Those are system-building responsibilities. A narrow prepared candidate removes that burden while
retaining an inspectable approval boundary and reusing the already-tested transactional chain.

The candidate must not infer environmental facts. The current owner profile demonstrates why:
"Home" alone does not prove that a stable chair or enough usable space is available. Missing facts
therefore appear as explicit blockers rather than an infeasible plan or a hidden assumption.

## Alternatives considered

- **Automatically create the demand after strategy creation.** Rejected because preparation and
  authority remain distinct and factual environment selection may be required.
- **Treat every home as having a chair and 2 m².** Rejected because equipment and space are changing
  environmental constraints, not athlete identity or universal defaults.
- **Ask the owner to fill the existing expert form.** Retained only as a collapsed recovery path;
  it is not the primary owner-alpha workflow.
- **Call ten minutes the minimum effective dose.** Rejected because the reviewed source does not
  establish that claim.
- **Add sets and repetitions now.** Rejected because dose requires a separate evidence, safety, and
  progression decision.
- **Derive candidate identity from projection time.** Rejected because harmless refreshes would
  make an unchanged candidate impossible to accept. Identity includes normalized source state but
  excludes the display clock.

## Assumptions and provisional choices

- One exact muscular-endurance DEVELOP priority is the only governed owner-alpha path.
- Two weekly scheduling slots follow the ratified frequency claim.
- Five minutes per slot is enough capacity for a later conservative prescription, but is not itself
  that prescription.
- Same-account approval remains provisional and is explicitly bound to the active reviewer
  assignment.
- The existing manual authoring workflow remains available for recovery and future expert use.

## Unresolved questions

- Existing environments cannot yet append a revised floor-space fact from the athlete PWA; this is
  the next usability blocker for profiles that omitted space during onboarding.
- What exact evidence and safety logic should govern the first sets, repetitions, effort, tempo,
  rest, technique cues, and stop rules?
- Should resource demands gain a typed supersession relationship rather than relying on distinct
  append-only identities and strategy history?

## Consequences

- The owner reviews a complete prepared candidate rather than writing technical planning inputs.
- Factual environment changes invalidate stale candidate acceptance without changing athlete
  identity.
- The resource chain remains distinct from both scientific claims and eventual performed work.
- No block, weekly plan, session prescription, or workout is created.
- The next coherent work is append-only environment-constraint reporting, followed by a prepared
  first block and separately governed first-session dose.
